Explore Beautiful Places in India

1. Agra

Agra is famous for the Taj Mahal.

Famous Attraction: Taj Mahal, Agra Fort

2. Goa

Goa is famous for beautiful beaches.

Famous Attraction: Baga Beach, Fort Aguada

3. Jaipur

Jaipur is known as the Pink City.

Famous Attraction: Hawa Mahal, Amber Fort

4. Mumbai

Mumbai is the financial capital of India.

Famous Attraction: Gateway of India, Marine Drive

5. Manali

Manali is famous for mountains and snow.

Famous Attraction: Solang Valley, Rohtang Pass
